Read these four caveats with the wall

  1. One toy design. An 8-bit lock (rtl/v2.v), modelled bit flips, 6 cycles, at most 3 flips. Not a chip, not physics, not a security proof.
  2. The settings are not independent. A full grid of 5 options on 3 Yosys builds (two share one git sha): 264 settings, only 152 distinct netlists.
  3. One option does most of it. 120 of the 180 one-flip settings come from the single option opt_merge.
  4. Not a new effect. Synthesis weakening redundancy was previously reported by SYNFI. Nothing here is established as new.

How to read the wall

One Verilog file (rtl/v2.v, three copies of the key check), 288 synthesis settings: 3 Yosys builds × 96 option combinations. 264 gave a netlist the solver could read; only 152 of those netlists are different.

A "3" means no attack was found with 2 or fewer flips; it does not mean safe. Hover, tap or focus a tile for its options, size, netlist hash and source row.

Step 1 of 4 The blueprint

One Verilog file: rtl/v2.v. The key check is written three times, so on paper a fault has to beat all three: 3 flips.

Now hand the same file to many builders.

Step 2 of 4 The builders

Same file, 288 synthesis settings: 3 Yosys builds × 96 option combinations (flow, order, ABC script, opt_merge, keep). 24 gave a netlist the engine cannot read. The other 264 built 152 different gate netlists.

How many still need 3 flips?

Step 3 of 4 The verdicts

For each netlist a solver searched for the fewest modelled bit flips that open the lock with the wrong key (6 cycles, up to 3 flips).

20 of 152 keep 3. 38 drop to 2. 94 open with a single flip.

Step 4 of 4 The cause

Outlined: settings that let the tool see the three identical checks together. It merges them into one copy; one flip then decides everything.

A second, smaller wall: one constant changed (V2 against V2e)

Different experiment, 8 flows, not the 264 above. These are the 8 project flows of --synth-sweep, run on two Yosys versions. V2e is V2 with one constant changed (armed value 0xCC to 0xA5), so that reaching the accept word touches all three check copies instead of two. V2e needs 3 flips on 6 of 8 flows on Yosys 0.33 and 6 of 8 on 0.38+92; V2 does on 0 and 3. The two flows that merge the checks still open with 1 flip in every row: the re-encoding does not fix merging. Only 9 of 253 possible constants were tried. In the every-setting view each block is a 6 × 6 grid: columns are the ABC script (default, no MUX, AND/OR only, -fast, -dff, no ABC), rows are Yosys build × keep attribute; dashed "–" tiles (24) are netlists the engine could not read: no verdict, not counted. In the distinct-netlist view a netlist is drawn once, at the first setting that built it, and in the cause step it counts as "opt_merge on" if any setting that builds it has the option on (6 netlists are built both with and without it).
