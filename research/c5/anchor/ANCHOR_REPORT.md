## 1. N10: z3-free brute-force anchors

### The simulator (`anchor_sim.py`)
Written fresh for this task. It shares no code with `b3pack.py`, `faultpack.py`, `modelsim.py`, `bf_b3.py` or `glitchsat.py` (own JSON reader, own topological order, own cycle function, own event enumeration; no z3 import). All 256 values of the constant input `sig` are simulated in parallel as 256-bit Python integers; a lane counts as an attack if `boot_word == 0x96` is observed at some cycle < T and `sig != 0xC3`. Init = netlist `init` attributes else 0 (the engines' rule). Enumeration is exhaustive: DFS over cycles with state sharing, every set of at most K events, 4 processes. Event kinds: `stem_flip` (xor of any gate output or flop Q in one cycle; same site set as the engines: 76 sites on V2 spike, 93 on V2 apt 0.33); rail dips as B3 defines them (all gates of the domain forced to 0 in that cycle, flops of the domain capture 0; flop Q untouched; rail_cone = gates in the fanout cone of one net; units from the `$flatten\<unit>.` cell-name prefix). Own re-derivation of the domain groups; the counts of events per cycle equal B3's `n_events` (rail_half 7, rail_unions 15, rail_cone 53 on V2 spike).

Netlists (sha256): `reference/spike_netlists/v2.json` 289355dc...1ec; apt 0.33 V2 `research/c4/b3/nl/v2_apt033.json` = `research/c4/b3_inputs/nl/v2_apt033.json` 03198073...a59a (the apt 0.33 V2 netlist for the stem-flip anchors, 85 gates, 8 flops).

### 1d. What the anchors do and do not show
- They show that the two z3 engines (glitchsat/product CLI for stem flips, b3pack for rail events) and a z3-free simulator agree on the listed V2 cells, including one positive 3-flip row with all 182 triples counted, the apt 0.33 2-flip row, and the 0/22 selective-dip counts. They check encoding errors (cycle semantics, init, goal timing, fan-out of a stem flip, dip semantics as I read them from B3's docstring).
- They do not check that the model is physical, and the simulator implements the same reading of B3's dip definition (AoN force-0 and D-capture AND 0); a mistake in that reading would be shared. T=6 rows (the headline horizon) are not enumerated: T=4 for k <= 2 and T=4 for k <= 3 (stem flip). The k=3 rail-event negatives (`>3`) are not brute-forced here. `boot_ok` rows: brute-force ok counts are recorded in the JSON (`ok_attacking_by_size`) but only compared with z3 in 1b.

## 4. N5: DA/REP grid versus E2's 864 configs
MEASURED by `python3 grid_reconcile.py` (output `grid_reconcile.out`). E2 V2 slice: 288 configs, 264 solved, 24 ENGINE_UNSUPPORTED; solved min flips 1/2/3 = 180/60/24. DA/REP V2 slice: 32 netlists (2 builds x 2 flows x 8 ABC invocations), unit min 2 for 26 and 3 for 6. Mapping (from `gen_rep.sh` and E2 `matrix.py`): same flow names and the same script tail; DA/REP recipe = E2's `order=abc_then_flatten`, `merge=nomerge`, `keep` as written. ABC overlap: REP s0 = E2 default, s1 = noMUX, s2 = fast, s4 = AND_OR; s3, s5, s6, s7 have no E2 level (E2 has `-dff` and no-abc, REP does not). So 16 of the 32 REP netlists have an E2 counterpart (24 REP/E2 pairs, because REP's conda build maps to E2's two conda builds, same git sha): the min flips agree 24/24 and the gate counts agree 24/24. In E2 that sub-grid is 24 configs with 18 min-2 and 6 min-3. The 6/32 and 26/6 are NOT wholly inside E2's counts: of REP's 6 min-3 netlists three (plain, conda, s0/s1/s4) are E2 configs (each present twice in E2 for formal/eda, and again for keep_off), the other three (plain, conda, s5/s6/s7) have no E2 level; of REP's 26 min-2 netlists 13 are in E2's grid and 13 are not. Nor do the two denominators match: E2's 24 of 264 (9.1 %, V2 min 3) lives in a grid where 180 configs reach 1 flip through `opt_merge`, flatten-before-ABC, or `abc -dff`; REP's recipe family has none of those factors, so it has no min-1 case (E2: 6 `-dff` configs in the same family, which REP lacks) and 6/32 (18.8 %) is a property of that one recipe family. One-paragraph statement: "The DA/REP 32-netlist grid and E2's 864-config grid share one recipe family (abc_then_flatten, no opt_merge, RTL keep as written, flows plain/opt, builds apt 0.33 / conda 0.38, ABC default/noMUX/fast/AND_OR); on the 16 shared netlists (24 pairs) the two give the same minimum flips and gate counts in 24 of 24; half of REP's 6/26 split lies outside E2's ABC levels; the figures 6/32 and 180/60/24 are counts in different grids and must not be compared as rates."
